Keeping Your Phoenix HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
